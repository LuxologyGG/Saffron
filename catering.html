03 / How It Works

Three steps to a full sofreh

01

Choose your spread

Pick the platter for ten, twenty, or thirty, or build around the family combos. Add a stews combo if your table runs on khoresh.

02

Call ahead

One call books it. Call (310) 504-0310 ahead, tell us the headcount, and the kitchen will have the spread ready when you are.

03

Pick up or delivery

Swing by the counter on Pacific Coast Hwy or have it delivered. Dine-in, pickup, delivery, and catering, whichever way your gathering leans.
