01/ The Kitchen

Kabab off the charcoal, stews that took their time, and rice crowned with saffron, the way the table has always been set.

From our counter on Pacific Coast Highway we cook a short, honest menu: koobideh and barg over live flame, herb-dense khoresh, and polo steamed until every grain stands on its own.
