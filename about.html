01/ New Management

From market shelves to the deli counter.

Saffron & Rice is the newest name on a familiar Torrance corner. Formerly Saffron Food Market, the space has traded its market shelves for a working kitchen: a Persian and Middle Eastern deli and catering counter at 3801 Pacific Coast Hwy.

The menu is short on purpose. Koobideh and barg kababs come off the charcoal. Gheyme, ghorme sabzi, and fasenjoon simmer low and slow for our combos. Saffron rice arrives piled with barberries, fava beans and dill, or green beans and tomato. A deli case sends out sandwiches on wrap or soft baguette.

We cook for the South Bay the way the South Bay gathers: weeknight dinners, office lunches, Nowruz spreads, and party platters that feed ten, twenty, or thirty.

02/ The Counter

Four stations, one sofreh.

Kabab

Koobideh, barg, shish, and boneless chicken, skewered and fired over charcoal.

Stews

Gheyme, ghorme sabzi, and fasenjoon, simmered slow and ladled into our combo platters.

Rice

Baghali polo, zereshk polo, and loobia polo, steamed the Persian way.

Deli

Sandwiches on wrap or soft baguette, from koobideh to kuku sabzi, kotlet, and kalbas.
