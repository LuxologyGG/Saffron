## What's worth re-skinning for a Torrance Persian deli
1. **Bilingual logotype lockup** — Perso-Arabic calligraphic wordmark with small Latin annotation underneath. For a deli: hand-drawn Farsi script "سفرون/Saffron"-style mark over a Latin label; instantly signals authenticity to the Iranian-American community (Torrance/South Bay has a large one) while staying legible to everyone else.
2. **Handwritten script accent layer** — a calligraphic script (their "Roses Please") laid over photography for emotional headlines ("Be Unique, Be Inspired" → e.g. "Noosh-e jan!"). Use sparingly: one script moment per page.
3. **Mono + script pairing** — DM Mono labels against a romantic script is a cheap, distinctive system; works for deli price tags/menu labels.
4. **Persian-word product naming** (Sarv, Takhmeh) — name menu items/sections with transliterated Persian words + English descriptor.
5. Muted analog-film photo grading — warm, nostalgic; suits deli counter/produce photography.
