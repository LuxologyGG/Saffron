01
Our Visionary

Giovanni
Bolandrini visionary behind the group

Born and raised in Italy, Giovanni Bolandrini is a dynamic entrepreneur known for his influential contributions to the fashion and hospitality industries. In his early 20s, Giovanni moved to London, where he established himself as a leading figure in the fashion world by owning one of the city’s most renowned boutiques. His keen sense of style and innovative approach quickly garnered attention, making his store a go-to destination for fashion enthusiasts. Initially making a name for himself in the fashion industry by styling various world-class celebrities, he built a strong reputation that extended beyond retail.

In 2008, Giovanni’s entrepreneurial spirit led him to expand beyond fashion, founding the Pier88 Group, a multifaceted company dedicated to creating exceptional dining experiences. Under his leadership, Pier88 Group has grown to encompass a diverse portfolio of restaurants, with Khufu’s Restaurant being one of the standout establishments that blend contemporary cuisine with rich cultural influences. In recognition of his impact on the luxury dining scene, Bolandrini was featured in Forbes Middle East’s list of “20 Leaders Behind Luxury Dining in the Middle East.”

Founder Note

From fashion to hospitality, Giovanni’s trajectory is defined by authorship, instinct, and a clear ability to shape environments people remember.

02
Meet The Chef

Meet Chef
Mostafa Seif innovation meets egyptian tradition

“The Blend Of Innovation And Egyptian Traditions Gave Birth To My Recipes.” Mostafa Seif, Winner of Top Chef Middle East 2018 and awarded Two Knives at the 2025 Best Chef Awards, is renowned for his exceptional talent and boundless passion for cooking. As a leading figure in new Egyptian cuisine and one of the top chefs in the Middle East, his innovative approach continues to exceed expectations. Chef Mostafa serves as a menu consultant and recipe developer, creating dishes that masterfully blend traditional flavours with contemporary flair, truly redefining the culinary landscape.

Together, Giovanni Bolandrini and Chef Mostafa Seif have crafted a one-of-a-kind menu driven by passion, creativity, and a deep love for gastronomy.

Shehab
El Din Head Chef

Shehab El Din leads the Khufus kitchen with a point of view shaped by competition, discipline, and a deep respect for flavor. Featured on MBC Top Chef Season 6 and named Young Chef of the Year 2025, he brings precision, restraint, and momentum to every menu.

Ahmed Alwani Sous Chef

Yasmine Metaki Junior Sous Chef
